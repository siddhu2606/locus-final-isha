What is inside

Trust scorer · rules + MLEvery reading scored 0 to 1 by hand-written checks and a LightGBM model; either can veto a bad one.
Off radar + self-checkTag silent for 3 s → flag to its point of contact. Beacon silent → repair alert.
Moving beaconsBeacons can be carried; LOCUS uses each new position and its uncertainty.
Height in 3DEvery beacon has a height, sent in its Bluetooth packet; 4+ beacons also estimate yours.
Accurate short rangeCalibrate each beacon at 10 cm, 50 cm, 1 m or 2 m. Two distances also fit the fade rate.
ISHA, voice or chat"Where is the nearest wheelchair?" Works offline, understands Hinglish.
Laptop by codeThe receiver phone shows a 6-digit code; type it on any laptop.
