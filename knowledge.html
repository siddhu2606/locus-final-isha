Note 6 · flip the idea

What it needs

Each rescuer’s phone knows where it stands (GPS outside, or L.O.C.U.S). The victim’s phone or wearable broadcasts BLE in SOS mode. Concrete blocks signals, so every reading is treated as noisy (σ = 2.5 m here).

Concept: the engine already takes anchor positions as input. Here they arrive live.
