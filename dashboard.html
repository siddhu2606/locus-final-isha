Trust scorer · rules + ML

Every reading from every beacon gets a trust score from 0 to 1. The position fix weights each beacon by trust², so a bad beacon is out-voted instead of dragging the dot.

How the score is computed, and what happens when a beacon dies 
rules       raw_r = tR · tJ · √tV          residual · jump · variance checks, each 0 to 1
ML          raw_m = LightGBM probability that the reading is clean (14 features)
rules + ML  raw   = (raw_r + raw_m) / 2,   or min(raw_r, raw_m) when either is below 0.25
every reading     trust = 0.55 · trust_prev + 0.45 · raw
no reading        trust = trust_prev − 0.8 · dt        about 1.2 s from 1 to 0
position fix      weight_i = trust_i²       share_i = weight_i / Σ weight
  • The dead beacon: its trust falls by 0.08 per missed reading, so it is ignored after about a second. One lost packet is normal, so it is not cut instantly.
  • The other beacons keep their trust: what changes is their share of the vote.
  • The cost shows in the uncertainty: the ellipse grows, more when the lost beacon was the one closest to the tag.

What the beacon phones sense

#StateStepsCompassHearsOn the map
–
Each beacon phone (Android app) listens to the other beacons and reads its own step counter and compass, and sends both inside its Bluetooth packet. When one is carried, its steps and heading guess where it went and its distances to the still beacons correct it, so it moves on the map by itself. Needs at least 2 beacons that stay put.
