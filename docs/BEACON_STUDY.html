How many beacons?

Simulation, 8 × 6 m room, walking target, RSSI from a log-distance model (n = 2.2, −59 dBm at 1 m) with Gaussian shadowing, packet loss and 8 dB body-blocking bursts. 24 random walks per row. LOCUS = particle filter with trust scoring; Plain LS = per-frame least squares. Reproduce: node scripts/sim_beacons.js.

What it says

  • 3 beacons in a triangle is a real improvement over 2 and is the practical minimum. 4 and 6 keep helping, with diminishing returns.
  • The gain from 3 to 4 is modest here (about 0.05 to 0.15 m median). The bigger benefit of 4 is that you can lose one and still have 3.
  • 3 collinear beacons (a line) are not better than 2 in the worst case: they cannot tell the two sides of the line apart without motion history. Do not place them in a row.
  • Noise matters far more than beacon count: going from 2 dB to 6 dB roughly doubles the error.
  • Caveat: this is simulated RSSI. Real rooms add reflections, walls and phone-orientation effects the model does not have.
